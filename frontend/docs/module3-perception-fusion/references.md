# References: Module 3 - Perception & Sensor Fusion

## Academic Sources

1. Thrun, S., Burgard, W., & Fox, D. (2005). *Probabilistic Robotics*. MIT Press. This foundational text covers sensor models, state estimation, and filtering techniques essential for understanding robot perception and sensor fusion.

2. Siciliano, B., & Khatib, O. (Eds.). (2016). *Springer Handbook of Robotics* (2nd ed.). Springer. Comprehensive reference covering all aspects of robotics including perception, sensor fusion, and humanoid robotics applications.

3. Barfoot, T. D. (2017). *State Estimation for Robotics: A Matrix Lie Group Approach*. Cambridge University Press. Advanced treatment of state estimation techniques including sensor fusion for mobile robots and humanoid systems.

4. Gonzalez, J., Fong, T., Memisoglu, D., So, Y., & Khatib, O. (2003). Humanoid robot simulation using choreonoid. *Proceedings 2003 IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems*, 3, 2403-2408. Discusses simulation approaches for humanoid robots with perception systems.

5. Coumans, E., & Bai, Y. (2016). Mujoco: A physics engine for model-based control. *IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS)*, 5726-5731. Covers physics simulation for accurate sensor data generation in robotics applications.

## Industry Standards and Best Practices

11. Murillo, A. C., & Sagues, C. (2017). Vision-based localization from the ground up. *IEEE Transactions on Robotics*, 33(1), 156-172. Provides insights into visual perception and sensor fusion for robotics applications.

12. Durrant-Whyte, H., & Bailey, T. (2006). Simultaneous localization and mapping: Part I. *IEEE Robotics & Automation Magazine*, 13(2), 99-110. Classic paper on sensor fusion for perception in robotics.

13. Luber, M., Tipaldi, G. D., Spinello, L., Nardi, D., & Kleiner, A. (2012). People detection and tracking using multiple cues and a particle filter on a humanoid robot. *2012 IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems*, 5223-5229. Example of perception on humanoid robots.

14. Rusu, R. B., & Cousins, S. (2011). 3D is here: Point Cloud Library (PCL). *IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA)*, 1-4. Key resource for point cloud processing in robotics perception.

15. Hornung, A., Wurm, K. M., Bennewitz, M., Stachniss, C., & Burgard, W. (2013). OctoMap: An efficient probabilistic 3D mapping framework based on octrees. *Autonomous Robots*, 34(3), 189-206. Essential for 3D perception and mapping in robotics.

## Simulation and Digital Twin Research

16. Rasheed, A., San, O., & Kvamsdal, T. (2020). Digital twin: Values, challenges and enablers. *IEEE Access*, 8, 21980-22012. Comprehensive overview of digital twin concepts and applications.

17. Lu, Q., Parlikad, A. K., Woodall, P., Ranasinghe, R., & McFarlane, D. (2021). Digital twin for maintenance: Literature review and propositions. *IFAC-PapersOnLine*, 54(1), 100-105. Discussion of digital twin applications in physical systems.

18. Zhu, K., & Vibhav, N. (2021). A survey of robotic simulation. *Applied Sciences*, 11(11), 5021. Survey of simulation approaches in robotics with focus on perception systems.

19. Waiau, T., et al. (2016). Digital Twin: Manufacturing Excellence through Virtual Factory Replication. *Procedia CIRP*, 55, 14-19. Early work on digital twin concepts relevant to robotics applications.

20. Kusiak, A. (2018). Smart manufacturing. *International Journal of Production Research*, 56(1-2), 508-517. Covers digital twin applications in complex physical systems.
